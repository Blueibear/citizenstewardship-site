Proposal Two: Feasible Democratic Reform Without a Full Rewrite

This draft focuses on reforms that could move forward without fully rewriting the Constitution.

Possible routes include federal statute, state law, congressional rules, agency enforcement, interstate compact, and targeted constitutional amendment.

Why this proposal matters

It offers concrete reform steps that can be debated and tested now rather than deferred indefinitely.

What makes this version more practical

It relies on available legal and institutional channels with fewer dependencies on full constitutional redesign.
