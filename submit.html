Submit an Idea

This site is static and has no backend form processing. Use the public board or email.

Submit through the public discussion boardEmail contact@citizenstewardship.org

Suggested format

  • What problem are you trying to solve?
  • What reform are you proposing?
  • Why is it necessary?
  • What are the immediate benefits?
  • What are the risks or downsides?
  • What are the long-term benefits?
  • Who would support it?
  • Who might oppose it?
  • What sources support your argument?

Not every suggestion will be adopted, but constructive criticism is welcome.
