About

This is citizen-created work. These proposals are drafts, not finished governing texts.

The goal is democratic repair, not partisan victory. A healthier system should be harder to buy, harder to corrupt, harder to lie through, and easier for ordinary people to understand.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. The point is not to pretend these reforms are easy. The point is to stop pretending the current system is working.
