What this is

This is a public review hub for two draft reform proposals. They are meant to be tested in public, challenged in public, and improved in public.

Why this exists

Many people can see dysfunction but struggle to find structured places to debate concrete alternatives. This site is meant to host that work in plain language.

Why something has to change

The current structure often rewards money, access, and information asymmetry more than public understanding and durable trust.

Why neither party gets a free pass

Both major parties have failed ordinary Americans by preserving systems that often protect insiders, donors, corporations, party machinery, and career politicians. This project is not about replacing one tribe’s control with another tribe’s control. It is about making public power harder to buy, harder to hide, harder to abuse, and easier for ordinary people to understand.

What you can do here

Read both drafts, download the PDFs, disagree openly, suggest revisions, and submit alternatives that may be stronger than what is here now.
